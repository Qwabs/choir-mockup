# Meto Dwom 2nd Edition — print notes

One-colour spot artwork for the Meto Dwom 2nd Edition apparel range. The chest mark is supplied alongside two back options: the treble clef above or below the METO DWOM wordmark. The **2ND EDITION** line is intentionally removed from the back.

## Artwork sizes

| Placement | Actual print | Artboard / file |
|---|---|---|
| Left chest mark | 3.25 in wide × 1.30 in high | PNG 3.5 × 2.0 in (1050 × 600 px); SVG 3.53 × 1.74 in |
| Back — note above | 8.6 in wide × 4.0 in high | PNG 9.0 × 4.41 in (2700 × 1324 px); SVG 8.84 × 4.33 in |
| Back — note below | 8.6 in wide × 4.37 in high | PNG 9.0 × 4.77 in (2700 × 1430 px); SVG 8.84 × 4.68 in |

All PNGs are transparent, 300 DPI, and supplied as flat solid colour. The SVGs use outlined text, a vector clef, and a single fill that can be changed to any spot colour.

## Placement

**Chest mark**
- Wearer's left.
- Centre of the mark 3.25 in from the garment centre line.
- 7 in below the high point shoulder / shoulder seam.

**Back studies**
- Both options are centred on the back panel.
- Top of the print block 3 in below the collar seam.
- Note above: treble clef above **METO DWOM**.
- Note below: **METO DWOM** above the treble clef.
- Wordmark top 2.65 in below the collar seam; the clef stays at the same height. No edition text on the back.

## Inks

| Shirt | Ink | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| White | Espresso | `#2A2119` |
| Green | Warm ecru | `#F1E5CD` |
| Blue | Antique gold | `#E0AE58` |

Colour references are digital approximations. Match to a physical Pantone / ink drawdown before production. One screen per shirt.

## Garments

Suggested blank: heavyweight garment-dyed cotton tee — Comfort Colors 1717 or a comparable local equivalent. Confirm the actual blank shade names against the physical fabric; garment-dyed lots vary slightly.

## Printing

- Print at 100% scale. Do not enlarge from the mockup.
- Keep the artwork at a single flat ink; do not add gradients or halftones.
- For a soft hand on garment-dyed cotton, water-based ink is preferable. Flash and cure according to the ink manufacturer's instructions.
- Do not print the mockup photography. Use only the chest and back artwork files.
